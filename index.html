Bazcore - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 21, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Bazcore handles information when used in Discord servers.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features enabled and used, Bazcore may process the following information:

  • Discord User IDs.
  • Discord Server IDs.
  • Server configuration and bot settings.
  • Commands and interactions sent to the bot.
  • Information required for moderation features.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate Bazcore's features.
  • Manage server configurations.
  • Perform moderation and administrative functions.
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse.
  • Improve bot performance and reliability.

3. Data Storage and Security

Information may be stored when necessary for the bot's functionality. We take reasonable measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except when necessary to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, or protect the security of Bazcore and its users.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law.

Users and server administrators may contact us to request access to or deletion of eligible stored information.

6. Discord

Bazcore operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page.
